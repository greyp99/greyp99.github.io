Pacific Rhododendron

About the Plant

Scientific Name: Rhododendron macrophyllum

Common Names: Pacific Rhododendron, Coast Rhododendron, Bigleaf Rhododendron

Physical Size

The Pacific Rhododendron typically grows 6 to 12 feet tall, but can reach up to 20 feet in ideal conditions.

Geographic Range

It grows along the Pacific Coast from British Columbia through Washington and Oregon into Northern California. It is the official Washington State flower.

Climate

This plant grows best in cool, moist coastal forests. It prefers partial shade and well-drained, acidic soil.
