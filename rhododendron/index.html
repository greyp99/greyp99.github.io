Pacific Rhododendron

About the Plant

Scientific Name: Rhododendron macrophyllum

Common Names: Pacific Rhododendron, Coast Rhododendron, Bigleaf Rhododendron

Physical Size

The Pacific Rhododendron typically grows 6 to 12 feet, but can reach up to 20 feet in ideal conditions.

Geographic Range

Rhododendrons grow around the Pacific Coast from British Columbia through Washington, Oregon and Northern California. It is also the official Washington State flower.

Climate

This plant grows best in cool and moist coastal forests. Rhododendrons prefer a bit of shade and well-drained, acidic soil.
