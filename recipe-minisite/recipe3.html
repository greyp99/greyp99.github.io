Fluffy Pancakes

Soft and fluffy breakfast pancakes.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Any choice of berries (optional)

Directions

  1. Place the flour, following with cracking the egg, putting the baking powder, sugar, and milk into a bowl.
  2. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl until smooth.
  3. Put in the desired berries you'd like (optional)
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour the batter onto the pan.
  6. Flip when bubbles form on top.
  7. Cook until golden brown and serve!
