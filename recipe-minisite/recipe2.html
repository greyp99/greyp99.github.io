Grilled Cheese Sandwich

A classic crispy and cheesy sandwich.

A flavorful food, easy to make at home

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread
  • 2 slices cheese of your choice
  • 1 tbsp butter

Directions

  1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Place cheese between unbuttered sides.
  3. Cook on a pan over medium heat.
  4. Flip when golden brown and cook the other side.
  5. Serve hot.
