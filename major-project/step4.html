Step 4: Assembling the Cake

This is the step where all of the separate parts come together to form the finished cake. Assembly involves stacking the cake layers, spreading the filling evenly, and making sure the cake stays stable. Taking your time during this step will help the final cake look cleaner and prevent the layers from sliding.

Layer the Cake

Place the first cooled cake layer onto a cake plate or flat serving plate. Make sure the cake is completely cool before starting, because warm cake can melt the whipped cream filling and make assembly messy.

Add the Filling

Spread an even layer of whipped cream over the first cake layer using a spatula. Then add a layer of cherries across the cream, spreading them evenly so each slice will have cherry filling. Avoid placing too much filling in one spot, since this can make the cake uneven.

Add the Second Layer

Carefully place the second cake layer on top of the filling. Press down gently so the layers stay together, but do not press too hard or the filling may spill out from the sides.

Tip for Keeping the Cake Stable

If the cake begins to slide or feels unstable, place it in the refrigerator for a short time to help the filling firm up. Spreading the filling evenly and not overloading the layers will help keep the cake balanced during decorating.
