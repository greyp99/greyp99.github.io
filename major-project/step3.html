Step 3: Making the Filling

The filling is what gives Black Forest cake its light, creamy texture and cherry flavor. This step focuses on preparing the whipped cream and cherries before the cake is assembled. It's important to get the texture right because the filling sits between the cake layers and helps hold everything together.

Whipped Cream

Pour the 2 cups of cold heavy whipping cream into a clean mixing bowl. Add 2 tablespoons of powdered sugar, then mix until the cream becomes thick and forms stiff peaks. Stiff peaks mean the whipped cream holds its shape when you lift the mixer or whisk. If the cream is too soft, it may slide out from between the cake layers.

Cherry Filling

Drain the cherries before adding them to the cake so the extra liquid does not make the cake soggy. If the cherries are large, cut them into smaller pieces so they spread more evenly across each layer. Save a few whole cherries for decorating the top of the cake in the later step.

Texture Tips

The whipped cream should look smooth, thick, and fluffy. If it still looks loose or runny, keep mixing for a little longer, but watch it carefully. Overmixing can make the cream grainy, so stop once it holds its shape. Keeping the cream cold will also help it stay firm while assembling the cake.
