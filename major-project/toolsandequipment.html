Tools & Equipment

Having the right tools prepared before baking will make the process much easier and more organized. Most of these tools are common kitchen items, and some can be substituted if needed.

Basic Baking Tools

  • 2 round cake pans
  • Large mixing bowls
  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Hand mixer or stand mixer
  • Rubber spatula
  • Whisk
  • Cooling rack
  • Oven mitts

Optional Tools

  • Cake turntable for decorating
  • Piping bag for whipped cream designs
  • Offset spatula for smoother frosting

Tips

If you do not have a stand mixer, a hand mixer will still work well for whipping cream and mixing batter. You can also line cake pans with parchment paper to help prevent the cake from sticking.
